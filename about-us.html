Last updated: 12 September 2026

Sarvasamriddhi is a platform offering books, educational programmes, Yoga Nidra practices, meditation guidance, and resources related to mental well-being and personal development.

Our purpose is to make practical guidance on managing stress, improving sleep, cultivating mental calmness, and developing a balanced lifestyle accessible to Kannada-speaking audiences.

The books and programmes featured on this website are created or presented by author and Yoga Nidra guide M. Gopalkrishna Bhat.
