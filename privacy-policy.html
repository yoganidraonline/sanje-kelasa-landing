Payments

Payments are processed securely by an authorised third-party payment gateway. We do not collect or store your complete card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or internet banking password. The payment service provider handles payment information under its own privacy and security policies.

We may share your name, mobile number, and shipping address with courier or delivery partners only to the extent required to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal information or share it with unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

We may retain order and transaction information for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, accounting, taxation, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

You may request correction of your personal information or ask us to delete information that we are not legally or operationally required to retain. Please send such requests to gkbhatssy@gmail.com.
